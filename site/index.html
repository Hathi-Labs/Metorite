Metorite projects a single platform to each audience — from a personal workspace to department centers to the leadership rollup — so your teams share one source of truth instead of a dozen disconnected tools.

Agents that do the work

Webhooks, schedules, and signals trigger specialist agents that execute tasks and self-heal on failure — no one babysitting a queue.

One platform, every department

Projects, workflows, knowledge, dashboards, and chat live in one product — pre-scoped inside each center or as standalone apps, with a single permission model.

Multi-tenant by design

Every organization is isolated at the database row with enforced row-level security. Your company's data is walled off by construction, not by convention.

Your tools, connected

Bring the systems you already run. Metorite mirrors and orchestrates across them so work flows without copy-paste between apps.

Visibility you control

Private, center, or org-wide — plus targeted group grants. Two clear axes decide who inside your company sees what, with no hidden third rule.

Auditable by default
