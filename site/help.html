Help: your connected accounts and your data

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This page tells you how to connect a mailbox to Metorite, how to disconnect it, how to remove Metorite's access at your mail provider, and how to delete your data. The Privacy Policy says what Metorite does with that data.

1. A Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox

Connect it

  1. Sign in at app.metorite.com and open Email.
  2. Choose Add email account, then choose Google / Gmail.
  3. Sign in at Google with the account you want to connect.
  4. Google shows the two permissions that Metorite asks for. Read them, then choose Allow.
  5. Back in Metorite, choose how much earlier mail to import: none, or up to the last six months.

Metorite asks for two Google permissions. gmail.modify lets it read, send and organise your mail, its labels and its drafts. gmail.settings.basic lets it read and manage your Gmail filters. Metorite never receives your Google password. The Google user data section of the Privacy Policy lists each feature that uses these permissions.

Turn AI features on or off

Open AI Settings in the Email app to turn automatic sorting and automatic drafts on or off. The inbox digest has its own settings. Automatic drafts, the digest and insights are off until you turn them on.

Disconnect it, and delete Metorite's copy

  1. Open Email in Metorite.
  2. Open the Account menu of the mailbox, and choose Disconnect mailbox.
  3. Confirm with Disconnect.

Metorite then stops reaching the mailbox, and deletes its stored copy of it at once: the messages, the attachment records, the labels, the drafts it saved, the rules, the AI drafts, the insights and the access tokens. Your mail stays in Gmail.

Remove Metorite's access in your Google Account

  1. Open myaccount.google.com/permissions and sign in.
  2. Choose Metorite in the list of apps.
  3. Remove its access.

Do this as well as disconnecting in Metorite. If you only remove access at Google, Metorite can no longer reach your mailbox, but it keeps its stored copy until you disconnect the mailbox in Metorite or ask us to delete it.

Ask us to delete your data

Write to info@hathilabs.com from the address of your Metorite account, and say what you want deleted. We confirm the request and complete it within 30 days.

2. A Microsoft 365 (Outlook) mailbox

Connect it in the same way: open Email, choose Add email account, choose Microsoft 365 / Outlook, and sign in at Microsoft. To disconnect it, open the Account menu of the mailbox and choose Disconnect mailbox. Metorite deletes its stored copy at once. To remove Metorite's access at Microsoft, open the apps page of your Microsoft account and remove Metorite.

3. A WhatsApp Business number

Open WhatsApp, then Numbers, and choose Disconnect on the number. Metorite deletes every message, chat, contact and attachment it stored from that number at once.

4. Your account and your organisation

An administrator can cancel the organisation's subscription. Sign-in then stays open for an export window, so you can take your data out. After that window we delete the organisation and all of its content. Backups roll over within 30 days.

5. Contact

For help with your data, write to Hathi Labs Private Limited at info@hathilabs.com.
