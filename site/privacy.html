Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Metorite is a company operating system provided as software as a service by Hathi Labs Private Limited. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. It applies to metorite.com, app.metorite.com and the Metorite application.

If you connect a Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox, section 3, Google user data (Gmail), explains exactly what Metorite does with the data it receives from Google.

1. Who we are

Metorite is operated by Hathi Labs Private Limited, a company registered in India ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for account and billing data. For the content our customers put into the product, the customer organisation is the controller and we act as a processor on their instructions.

Questions about this policy: info@hathilabs.com. You can also write to privacy@metorite.com.

2. What we collect

Account and sign-in data

  • Your email address, and your display name if your sign-in provider supplies one.
  • A one-time sign-in code, or an authentication token from Google if you sign in with Google. We never receive or store your Google password.
  • Records of sign-in events, used for security and support.

Organisation and billing data

  • Your organisation name and its short identifier.
  • Your registered state and, if you supply one, your GSTIN, both used for tax purposes.
  • The number of seats you ask for, the seats assigned to you, your plan, and your subscription status.
  • Usage counts for billing, including AI usage recorded against your organisation.

Content you put into the product

Metorite stores the work you do in it. Depending on which parts you use, that can include tasks and projects, notes, calendar entries, meeting records and transcripts, customer records, and messages and attachments from accounts you choose to connect, such as a Gmail, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 mailbox. We do not inspect this content except as needed to run, support and secure the service.

A mailbox you connect

You can connect a Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox, or a Microsoft 365 (Outlook) mailbox, to the Email app. You connect it yourself, at your mail provider's own sign-in page, and you can disconnect it at any time. A mailbox you connect is private to you: other members of your organisation cannot read it in Metorite. For a Google mailbox, section 3 below gives the full detail.

A WhatsApp Business account you connect

If you connect a WhatsApp Business account, you do it through Meta's own sign-in window, and you choose the business and the phone number. Metorite then receives, through the WhatsApp Business Platform:

  • the messages that number sends and receives, with their attachments, voice notes and delivery status;
  • the names and phone numbers of the people it talks to, and the contacts saved in the WhatsApp Business app;
  • if you allow it, up to six months of earlier one-to-one chats, which Meta lets the business share when it connects.

We use this only to show your WhatsApp conversations inside Metorite, to sort them, to draft replies for you, and to answer your questions about them. We do not send a message unless you or someone in your organisation chooses to send it. We do not use WhatsApp data for advertising, and we do not sell it. Group chats are not shared with us.

What we do not collect

  • We do not use advertising trackers, and this marketing site sets no cookies and runs no scripts.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we never have.
  • We do not collect special category data on purpose. Please do not put it into free text fields.

3. Google user data (Gmail)

This section applies when you connect a Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox to Metorite. On Google's consent screen the app is named Metorite, and Hathi Labs Private Limited operates it. This section adds to the rest of this policy. Where they differ about data that Metorite receives from Google APIs, this section applies.

Limited Use. Metorite's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI and machine learning. Google Workspace APIs are not used to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI and/or ML models.

3.1 How you connect, and the permissions Metorite asks for

You choose to connect a Google mailbox in the Email app. You then sign in at Google, and Google shows you the permissions before you agree. We never receive or store your Google password. Metorite asks for exactly two Google permissions, and no others:

Google permissionWhat it lets Metorite doThe features that need it
gmail.modifyRead your messages, their attachments, your labels and your drafts. Send mail and save drafts. Add and remove labels, and move mail to the archive, spam or trash. Read your mailbox address and its change history, so that Metorite stays in step with Gmail.The inbox and reading pane, search, sending and replying, drafts, triage and rules, labels, the inbox digest, AI drafting, and the email assistant.
gmail.settings.basicRead your Gmail filters, and create or remove a filter.The filter list in the Email app, and "block sender", which adds a Gmail filter for a sender that you choose.

When you delete a message in Metorite, it goes to your Gmail Trash, where Gmail's own rules apply. When you discard a draft, Gmail removes it, as Gmail itself does. Metorite does not access your Google Contacts, Calendar, Drive or any other Google service through this connection.

3.2 What Google data Metorite accesses

  • Message headers: the sender, the recipients, the subject and the date.
  • Message bodies: the text and the formatted body of each message.
  • Attachments: the name, type and size of each attachment, and its content when you open it or ask the AI to read it.
  • Labels: Gmail's system labels, such as Inbox, Sent and Spam, and the labels that you created.
  • Drafts: the drafts in your mailbox, and the drafts that you or Metorite save there.
  • Settings and filters: your Gmail filters, through gmail.settings.basic.
  • Mailbox details: the address of the mailbox, and Gmail's change history, which tells Metorite what changed since the last sync.

3.3 How Metorite uses it

We use Google user data only to provide the user-facing email features that you see and use in Metorite. We do not use it for any other purpose. The features are:

  • Your inbox and two-way sync. We show your mail in the Email app, and we keep it in step with Gmail in both directions. When you read, label, archive or move a message in Metorite, the same change happens in Gmail.
  • Search. We search your stored mail when you ask.
  • Sending and drafts. We send mail only when you tell us to: when you send a message, or when you turn on the inbox digest, which Metorite then sends from your mailbox to you. The digest is off until you turn it on. A rule can save a reply or a forward as a draft, and it stays a draft until you send it.
  • Triage and rules. We sort new mail, add the labels that your rules name, and mark threads that need your reply. You write the rules, and you can turn automatic sorting off.
  • Labels and filters. We apply your Gmail labels, and we list your filters. When you block a sender, we add a Gmail filter for that sender.
  • AI drafting. When you ask for a draft, or turn on automatic drafts, the AI writes a reply in your style. Automatic drafts are off until you turn them on. Nothing is sent until you send it.
  • The email assistant. When you ask the assistant a question, it reads the mail it needs to answer you.
  • Insights. If you turn on insights, Metorite notes facts from your mail, such as due dates and amounts, for you to see.

The AI features process your mail automatically to produce a result for you. A person does not read it to do this. The style that the AI learns for your drafts is personal to you and to that mailbox, and it is deleted when you disconnect the mailbox.

3.5 What we do not do with Google user data

  • We do not sell it.
  • We do not use it, or transfer it, to serve advertising, including personalised, retargeted or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not transfer it to data brokers or information resellers.
  • We do not use it to decide whether you are creditworthy, or for any lending purpose.
  • We do not use it, and we do not transfer it to anyone, to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models. We send it to the AI providers in section 3.4 only to get a result back for you, and never so that they can train or improve their models.

3.6 Who can read it

A mailbox that you connect is private to you. Other members of your organisation cannot read it in Metorite, unless you choose to share a message, for example by turning it into a task in a shared project.

Our staff do not read your Google user data, except in these cases:

  • you give us your explicit consent to read specific messages, for example to help with a support request;
  • it is necessary for security purposes, such as investigating a bug or abuse;
  • it is necessary to comply with applicable law; or
  • the data is aggregated and anonymised, and we use it for our internal operations.

3.7 How it is protected

  • In transit. All data moves between Google, Metorite, our suppliers and your browser over encrypted TLS connections.
  • At rest. Our database host encrypts the stored data at rest.
  • Access tokens. The OAuth tokens that Google gives us for your mailbox are encrypted by Metorite with a separate key before we store them. We never store your Google password.
  • Isolation. Each organisation's data is isolated in the database, and the database itself enforces that isolation.
  • Staff access. Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it.

3.8 How long we keep it

  • What we store. When you connect, you choose how much earlier mail to import: none, or up to the last six months. After that, Metorite keeps a copy of new mail while the mailbox stays connected. It also stores your labels, the drafts it saves, your rules, and the results of the AI features.
  • Older formatted mail. For a message older than 90 days, Metorite may keep only its text, and fetch the formatted version from Gmail again when you open it.
  • Short-lived caches. When you open an attachment, Metorite may keep the file, and its text, in a cache on our servers for up to one hour.
  • How long. We keep the copy while the mailbox stays connected. We delete it when you disconnect the mailbox, as section 3.9 describes.
  • Backups. Our backups hold a copy for up to 30 days, until they roll over.

3.9 How to revoke access, and how to delete your data

Disconnect the mailbox in Metorite.

  1. Sign in at app.metorite.com and open Email.
  2. Open the Account menu of the mailbox, choose Disconnect mailbox, and confirm.

When you disconnect, Metorite stops reaching the mailbox at once. It deletes, at once, its stored copy of that mailbox: the messages, the attachment records, the labels and folders, the drafts it saved, the rules, the AI drafts, the insights, the learned patterns and the encrypted access tokens. It then deletes the drafting memory of that mailbox. Your mail stays in Gmail.

Remove Metorite in your Google Account. Disconnecting in Metorite does not remove Metorite from your Google Account's list of connected apps. To remove it there too, open myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Metorite, and remove its access. If you remove access only at Google, Metorite can no longer reach your mailbox, and it shows the mailbox as needing to reconnect. Its stored copy stays until you disconnect the mailbox in Metorite, or ask us to delete it.

What outlives a disconnect. A task, a note or a chat message that you made from an email stays until you delete it, or until your organisation is deleted. Backups roll over within 30 days.

Ask us to delete it. Write to info@hathilabs.com from the address of your Metorite account. We confirm the request and complete it within 30 days. If your organisation cancels its subscription, we delete all of its data after the export window described in section 7.

The help page gives these steps one by one.

7. How long we keep it

  • Sign-in codes expire ten minutes after we issue them and are removed once used.
  • Account and content data is kept while your organisation has an account with us.
  • A connected mailbox is kept while it stays connected, and deleted when you disconnect it. Section 3.9 gives the detail.

  • Backups roll over within 30 days.
  • Billing and tax records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

8. How we protect it

  • Traffic to and from the service is encrypted with TLS.
  • Each organisation's data is isolated at the database level, and that isolation is enforced by the database itself rather than only by application code.
  • The access tokens for a mailbox you connect are encrypted before we store them.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it.
  • We do not store passwords for Metorite itself. Sign-in is by one-time code or by Google.

If your data sits inside a customer organisation's workspace, send your request to that organisation first. We will help them answer it.

10. Deleting your data

You can delete data yourself, or ask us to delete it. The help page gives the steps.

Disconnect a Gmail, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 mailbox

Open Email in Metorite, open the Account menu of the mailbox, and choose Disconnect mailbox. Metorite deletes its stored copy of that mailbox at once. For a Google mailbox, section 3.9 says exactly what is deleted, and how to remove Metorite's access in your Google Account.

Disconnect a WhatsApp Business account

  1. Sign in at app.metorite.com and open WhatsApp, then Numbers.
  2. Choose Disconnect on the number.

Disconnecting deletes, at once, every message, chat, contact and attachment that Metorite stored from that number. You can also remove Metorite's access on Meta's side, in the business settings of your WhatsApp Business account.

Delete your account or your organisation

An administrator can cancel the organisation's subscription. After the export window described in section 7, we delete the organisation and all of its content.

Ask us to delete it

Write to info@hathilabs.com from the email address of your Metorite account, and say what you want deleted. We confirm the request and complete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep a record, such as tax records.
