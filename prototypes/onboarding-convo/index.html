What we learned from other products

Eleven products, studied for 15 minutes. The sources are in RESEARCH.md.

What works

  • One open question first. Boardy asks what you work on, and Lovable asks what you want to build. The answer starts the work.
  • Value before the account. Duolingo gives a lesson before signup. Lovable keeps your prompt through it.
  • Ask for a permission when it pays, and say why. Granola reads the calendar "to start and stop on time".
  • Never a blank page. Notion fills the page from one answer. Granola puts a sample meeting in the list.
  • Stay in the trusted channel. Boardy uses phone and email. Tally sends the bill in WhatsApp.

What fails

  • A connection wall before any value. Cleo needs the bank first, with no manual path.
  • Slow first value. Boardy takes hours to a match. A busy owner forgets.
  • Vague in, weak out. Short answers give weak results, so Aroh must ask a sharp follow-up.
  • Warm that oversteps. A "personal" note about a user's photo forced a public apology.
  • A human step alone. Superhuman's 1:1 calls did not scale until a self-serve path joined them.
