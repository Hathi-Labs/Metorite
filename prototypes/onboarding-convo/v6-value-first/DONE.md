# v6 value first: done

## What it does

1. The first screen asks for one thing: the company website or name.
2. Aroh reads four public sources, with a visible tick for each one. Then it
   shows two cards. The first is a **draft profile**, and each field names its
   source. A guess says "estimate". The second is a **sample morning brief**
   for tomorrow at 7:30. Two lines are public and open. Three lines are
   blurred and locked, and each lock names the source that fills it, for
   example "Connect email to see which 3 threads wait for you".
3. The conversation runs beside the brief on a laptop, and in a dock under the
   brief on a phone. The first question comes after the first value: "It is a
   draft. What did I get wrong?" A correction updates the profile field, and
   its source changes to "You said".
4. Each connection uses a mock consent sheet. After it, the locked line opens
   with a dawn reveal: the blur clears, a yellow glow rises, and a "New" tag
   shows. Aroh then gives the insight in a yellow bubble.
5. The owner can click a locked line at any time, out of order. The chat then
   skips that step ("Your books are in already").
6. The payoff is the first brief on the dawn sky: "Good morning, Rajesh", with
   three decisions, each with its source chip and an action. Each skipped
   source puts a public decision in its place, so the brief always holds
   three. The brief then offers "Add any time" for each missing source.

Typed words are used: the business name or website, the person's name, and
the counts of people and buyers ("we are 38 staff and 250 buyers").

## The one idea it tests

Show the reward first, and let each connection earn itself. The owner sees
the shape of tomorrow's brief before Aroh asks a single question, and each
lock says exactly what a connection buys.

## What is weak

- The public facts are the Kaveri facts for every business name. A typed
  name changes the title and the chat, not the facts.
- On a phone, the dock takes about half of the screen until the payoff. Then
  it shrinks, and "Show chat" opens it again.
- "Tell me about your business" became "What did I get wrong?". This is a
  deliberate change from the shared arc, and some viewers can miss the open
  question.
- No real reading of a website happens. A real build needs a fast and honest
  scrape, and a clear "I could not find you" path. This demo has no such path.

## How to demo it in 2 minutes

1. Click **kaveritextiles.in**. Let the four ticks run (3 s). Point at the
   draft badges and the source under each field.
2. Point at the locked lines: "It shows what each connection buys."
3. Click **We are 42 people…**, then **Rajesh**, then **Both**. The profile
   fields flash and say "You said".
4. Click **Connect Gmail**, then **Allow**. Watch the line open with the dawn
   glow.
5. Out of order: click the locked **WhatsApp** line in the brief. Pick the
   groups. Then the chat asks for books: click **Tally Prime**, **Connect**.
6. Click **With the detail**. The first brief comes up on the dawn sky.
7. To show "no penalty", click **Restart** and skip everything. The brief still
   holds three decisions, from public facts.
