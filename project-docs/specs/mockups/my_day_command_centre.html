Design proposal · task 1946 · for the owner's decision

My Day becomes the place where you run your day

Capture, plan, start work, focus and close the day from one page. Calendar stays the place for deep planning, and Projects stays the place for the structure of the work. All three read and write the same tasks, the same time blocks and the same focus sessions, so nothing needs a sync.

Written 2026-11-07 from a map of the code on main and a study of the open-source app Stride, which you shared. The mockups use example tasks. Nothing here is built yet.

The idea

One loop, five verbs, one rule for each

A day of work follows one loop: you capture what comes in, clarify it, put it on time, do it, and close the day. Today each verb lives in a different app, and some cannot run from My Day at all. The proposal gives each verb one rule and one component, and lets every surface use the same one.

CaptureGet it out of your headOne capture box on My Day, ⌘K, the chat and Projects. Everything lands in your Inbox (D170). A time such as "at 3 pm" also makes a block.
ClarifyDecide what it is"Clarify 5" on My Day opens a quick pass over the Inbox. Projects keeps the full Inbox and the project tree.
PlanPut it on timePick today's top 3 and a One Thing, then "Fit my day" proposes blocks. You approve once. Calendar keeps the week and the full grid.
DoStart, focus, finish▶ on any task or block starts one focus session. The timer follows you into every app. At the end it asks "Is it done?".
CloseEnd the day cleanlySee what got done and the time you focused. Roll the rest over, and choose tomorrow's One Thing.

My Day changes with the time of day

In the morning it helps you plan. During the day it shows what you do now and what comes next. In the evening it helps you close. It picks the phase from the clock and your day state, and you can switch by hand. Try the three tabs.

What exists

Most of the parts are built. They sit in the wrong place.

Built, to move onto My Day

  • built The global focus session and its dock (FocusMode.tsx, mounted in AppShell), with 25/5, 50/10 and Flow. Only the Calendar can start it today.
  • built The now/next bar, "Fit what's left", "Rebuild my day", the startup ritual, the end-of-day review and the ★ One Thing, all in Calendar.
  • built The planner routes (plan, replan, rollover) and the day state.
  • built Quick Capture with sweep, the AI split and "Remind me". Today it opens only inside My work.
  • built The feed's in-place acts, the Reminders band and the AI waiting group.
  • built, dark The 07:30 brief card and the events store.

Missing or in conflict

  • rule navigation_shell.md §4.3 says My Day only notices, and Calendar owns start, focus and end of day. Your ask changes that rule.
  • rule Two "Today" rules: My Day reads time blocks, and My work reads due and start dates. They disagree.
  • No capture on My Day. The command bar's "New task" only opens Projects.
  • The focus room reads a store that My Day never loads, and its capture button no longer works.
  • Actual time is one start and end pair per task, and a new session overwrites it. No session log, no pauses, no auto-stop.
  • No "top 3" or star on a task. Only the One Thing.
  • The chat cannot plan or roll over the day yet (CAL-1 to CAL-6 are in Backlog).
Your decisions

Eight choices, each with a recommendation

Approve all eight, or change any of them on task 1946. After that I write the spec section and the D-numbers, and make one task for each slice.

MD-Q1 · the job of My Day

Recommended: My Day becomes the command centre: notice, plan, start, focus and close. Calendar keeps the week, the full grid and events. Projects keeps the structure. This amends navigation_shell.md §4.3.

Other: keep My Day as "notice only", and add links into Calendar.

MD-Q2 · one Today

Recommended: one rule for Today: the One Thing, a top 3 that you pick, what is on today's calendar, and what is due or starts by today. Every surface reads one helper.

Other: keep the two rules, and label them differently.

MD-Q3 · the top 3

Recommended: a "today's focus" mark on a task, with a cap of 3 that you can set from 1 to 10. The One Thing is one of them, with a gold star.

Other: the One Thing only, with no cap list.

MD-Q4 · phases

Recommended: My Day changes by phase: Plan until you start the day, Do while you work, Close after 17:30 or when you ask. A switch lets you pick one.

Other: one fixed layout all day.

MD-Q5 · the focus log

Recommended: a new table of focus sessions, one row per session, private to you, scoped to the organization. It holds the task, start, end, pauses and the answer to "Is it done?". Only you see your own minutes.

Other: also show the total minutes per task to the project's people.

MD-Q6 · the board

Recommended: starting focus on a company task in a To do lane moves it to In progress on its board, with Undo. "Done" in the check-in closes it there too.

Other: focus never changes the board.

MD-Q7 · capture with a time

Recommended: a capture always goes to the Inbox. If it names a time ("at 3 pm"), Metorite also makes a block or a reminder, and the toast offers Undo.

Other: the Inbox only, and you schedule it later.

MD-Q8 · the phone

Recommended: one column in this order: the running session, the capture and Ask bar at the thumb, today's plan, then what needs you. The Today card merges into the plan.

Other: keep the Today card first, as it is now.
