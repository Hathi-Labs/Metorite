## What

🔨 **BUILT, dark, preview only (2026-11-15, task 2358).** **The step player on sample data.** Rung 6. Wave 1 (D202). One step on each screen of a run. A run is a parent record, and its steps are line records (C13) with the fields `order`, `title`, `instruction` (richText), `photo` (file), `check` (checkbox, the required check) and `done`. **The model** is `lib/studio/stepPlayerModel.ts`. It sorts the steps by `order` (at most 100 rows), `resumeAt` gives the first step that is not done, and it gives the words `Step N of M`. **The gate:** a step with a required check does not pass while the check is not ticked, and the model refuses without a door call. **The write** is ONE `door.run("object:<step>.update", {id, data})`. `data` holds the result keys only (`done` and an optional note). It never holds an identity key, because the store stamps `updated_by` and `updated_at` from the session (R5). The sign-off line `Signed by <name> at <time>` reads those two from the row meta. **The view** is `components/studio/StudioStepPlayer.tsx`. It draws Back, Next, `ProgressBar`, the title, the instruction, the photo through `fileAddress` and `safeAddress` (a step with no photo draws a placeholder), the check as `Checkbox`, and the sign-off through `FormView`. A refused press shows a plain alert. A door answer `ok:false` keeps the member on the step and shows the text from `readRefusal`. A door with no update action (a viewer) gets the steps and their results, no sign-off and no `run` call. A row that lacks a field draws that part empty. The done chip takes its hue from `statusAccent`. **The sample** is `lib/studio/sampleSteps.ts`: 10 steps, one with no photo and one with a required check, and a door that stamps the sign-off. **Preview:** a Steps section on `/studio/blocks-preview`. **Not in D14a:** a catalog token, a route, a flag, a door change, timers, and `realDoor.ts`, which drops the row meta. A reload opens the same step: sample data resets on reload, so `resumeAt` tests hold it with 0, 4 and 10 steps done
