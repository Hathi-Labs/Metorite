# K3

Tier: STD

Gate: AGENT-SAFE

## What

**First-party template: inventory and procurement** (D146.6, §3.7 example). Items with product lines and units, locations, the stock ledger, vendors, purchase requests and purchase orders with an approval chain by amount, and goods receipt with a QC step. It posts to Tally or Zoho Books through a connector

## Done when

It installs in one step and passes D12. A receipt writes stock moves that keep stock on hand right. A purchase order above the limit waits for a director
