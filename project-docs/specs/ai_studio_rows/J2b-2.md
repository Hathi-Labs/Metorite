## What

🔨 **BUILT, dark (2026-11-13, task 3089).** **The share with people.** `POST /studio/packages/{id}/share` with the body `{"people": [address, ...]}` (`routes/studio/packages.py::share_package`). It ships behind `STUDIO_ORGS` and `PACKAGE_HOST_ORGS`. Either switch off gives 404 with no session. The right is `studio:publish` (D180.3). A member without it gets 403 `forbidden` before any session and any body read. The body is at most 20 KiB (413). Then one session. `_owned_private_install` holds the `apps` row `FOR KEY SHARE` first, the lock order of a publish. A package of another member or org is 404. An owner with no private install is 409 `private_only`. Then `promotion.share_with_people` takes the org lock and the row lock, and writes one `app_grants` row for each person, with `granted_by` the session member. The sharer comes from the session, never from the body (R5). A repeat is idempotent. A store refusal is its `detail_for`: 422 for a bad list (`bad_people`, `no_people`, `too_many_people` at 50, `not_a_person`, `own_address`, `not_a_member`), 409 `not_private`, `not_active` or `not_kept`, 403 `not_owner` and 404 `install_not_found`. `shared_by_another_owner` is 409, and the whole call writes no row. One `app_audit` row (kind `action`, op `package.share`, the install, the count and the addresses) commits with the share. The answer is 200 `{people: [...]}`. A person then reads the installed version as a viewer of J2a. ⚠️ No route removes one person. The store holds only `_UNSHARE_SQL`, which removes every share row of an install, so a per-person remove needs a new store writer first. No chat twin yet (J2b-3) and no screen. No migration and no new flag
