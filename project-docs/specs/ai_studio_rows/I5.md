## What

🔨 **BUILT, dark: the backend seam only (2026-11-11).** **The Knowledge link** (task 2402). Wave 1 (D202). A field or a step cites a section of a Knowledge page: a manual, an SOP, a safety data sheet. The app shows the section in place, and reads it through the Knowledge door as the viewer **Built:** `acb_common/studio_knowledge_link.py` (the link shape `{page_id, heading?, title}`, its validation, the cut of a section by heading, and a cap of 20,000 characters) and `gateway/studio/knowledge_link.py` (`read_link(db, user, link)`). The reader calls `routes/knowledge/core.load_page`, the one Knowledge visibility predicate, as the viewer. A viewer who can open the page gets `readable: true`, the live title and the section text. Any other viewer gets `readable: false`, the stored title and `text: null`, and the shape does not tell a page with no access from a page that is gone or in another org. **Not built:** no route, no step player (C16) and no field (C-series), so nothing calls the reader yet. The step shows the section when C16 calls `read_link`. Tests: `test_studio_knowledge_link.py` (hermetic) and `test_studio_knowledge_link_r8.py` (two orgs, real database).

## Done when

A step shows its manual section. A viewer who cannot open the page sees its title and no text
