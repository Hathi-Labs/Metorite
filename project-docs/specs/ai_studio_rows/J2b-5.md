# J2b-5

Tier: HIGH

Gate: AGENT-SAFE. Before any flag flip of the share route

## What

**The per-person unshare (task 3103).** A store writer removes one person from the share of an install, and a `DELETE` route calls it. The store holds only `_UNSHARE_SQL` today, which removes every share row of an install. This slice comes before any flag flip of the share route of J2b-2

## Done when

The owner removes one person, and the other people keep the app. A member who is not the owner gets 403 or 404. Org B cannot remove a share of org A. Fences: a hermetic test and an R8 test, in both `--must-run` lists
