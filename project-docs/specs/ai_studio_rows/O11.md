# O11

Tier: STD

Gate: AGENT-SAFE. After O5, C6, CE-4 and MCP-P4. D145 preview

## What

**Bookings**: resources, schedules, slots computed on read, a hold with an expiry (C6 and an E3 schedule), no double booking (a C1b `unique` rule), the Calendar door, reminders to the member (WS-66) and to the contact (O9). A `slots` block, and a clinic template

## Done when

Two guests who take the same slot at once give one booking. An unpaid hold frees its slot at its expiry. The booking shows on the member's calendar
