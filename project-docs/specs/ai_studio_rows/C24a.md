# C24a

Tier: STD

Gate: AGENT-SAFE. After C14

## What

🔨 **BUILT, dark (2026-11-14).** Task 2355. **Measurements with units and ranges, the flag.** Wave 2 (task 2327). Rung 4, beside D17a. No migration, no flag and no new field type. It extends the `quantity` of C14 with three keys of the field spec: `normal` and `critical`, each `{"low": "<decimal>"\|null, "high": "<decimal>"\|null}` in the base unit (the first of `units`), and `conversions`, a list of `{"from", "to", "factor"}`. A bound is inclusive. `critical` must hold `normal` (`critical.low` at most `normal.low`, `normal.high` at most `critical.high`). A factor is positive decimal text with at most 6 decimals. A list holds at most 30 conversions. `from` and `to` are listed units, `to` is the base unit (no chain), and a pair appears once. The value stays `{"value", "unit"}`. The flag is computed on read and never stored, so a change of a range rewrites no data: `quantity_flag` in `acb_common/studio_fields.py` converts to the base unit with exact decimals (`Decimal` with 60 digits, and `BigInt` at scale 10^12 in the twin `quantityFlag` of `fieldCheck.ts`, never a float), then answers `critical_low`, `critical_high`, `low`, `high` or `normal` (`critical` first). No range gives `none`, and a unit with no conversion gives `unchecked`. The words are Low, High, Critical low, Critical high and Not checked, and never Safe. The Table cell and the Detail sheet say them through `scalarText`, the Detail draws a chip from `statusAccent.ts`, and the Form shows a live line under the input. The golden file `studio_field_cases.json` holds 25 flag cases for both twins. **The flag is advice, not a diagnosis.** Deferred: a global unit library, chains, temperature offsets (C to F needs an offset, not a factor), presets for each analyte, convert-on-write, and a colour chip in a Table cell (it needs a new `DataGrid` cell kind)
