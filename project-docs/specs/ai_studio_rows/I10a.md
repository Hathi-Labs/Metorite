# I10a

Tier: HIGH

Gate: AGENT-SAFE. After I8b-1a

## What

**The break kind** (task 3300). An `on_break` hook of the registry writes a `broken` row of `integration_attention` (§8.2) when a row leaves `connected`. The Reconnect card and its words, and the Needs-you item of the owners. A reconnect clears the row

## Done when

A revoke makes one `broken` row and one Reconnect card for each install. A reconnect clears both. R8: a break of org A makes no row of org B
