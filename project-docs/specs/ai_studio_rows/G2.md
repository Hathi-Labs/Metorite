## What

🔨 **BUILT, dark (2026-11-01).** The interview and the plan card (§6.2 steps 1 and 2). Two tools join the `studio` pack. `studio_interview` checks at most five questions, one topic each (`who`, `records`, `next`, `approver`, `systems`). It refuses a repeated topic and a topic that the brief states. Its result is the exact input of `ask_questions`, which stays the one ask seam. `studio_plan` checks the manifest and shows one card with `request_confirmation`. The card draws each name from the manifest inside « », and the member rejects it to build nothing. The model cannot call that function, so the tool calls it and returns a `decision`: `approved`, `revised`, `stopped` or `no_card`. `stopped` means the card closed with no approval: a Reject, a timeout or an answer by another member. `no_card` means the chat shows no card. The plan text stays in `context`, and the member answers in words. A child agent never holds `studio_plan` (it is in `DISPLAY_TOOLS`). The card shows objects, views, workflows, skill, connections and actions. "Ask only what the brief does not say" is model judgement, and it is **advisory**. The build cost is **not priced yet**: no credit estimator exists, and J3 sets the caps. The card shows the count of parts and the line "Build cost: not priced yet. Credit limits come with a later release." It shows no price and no turn count
