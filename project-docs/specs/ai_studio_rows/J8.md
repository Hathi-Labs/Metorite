# J8

Tier: HIGH

Gate: AGENT-SAFE. After C17

## What

**E-signatures** (task 2406). Wave 2 (D202.5). A signature for regulated work: the member signs in again at the act, the record states what the signature means, and the record locks (C17)

## Done when

A signed revision cannot change. The signature names the member, the time and its meaning. A failed second sign-in writes nothing
