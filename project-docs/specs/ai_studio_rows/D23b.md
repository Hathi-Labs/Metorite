# D23b

Tier: HIGH

Gate: **OWNER-GATE** for the flip: `work_plan.md` §6, "any deploy that changes auth behaviour". The build is AGENT-SAFE. After O3 and D23a. D145 preview

## What

**The member session on a paired tablet** (task 2367). Wave 2 (D202). After O3 and D23a. A paired tablet on a shop floor shows the floor view of D23a, with no shell. A badge scan or a PIN opens a short session for one member, and each act names that member. O3 serves guests, and D23b serves members. **Questions (defaults, open on task 2367):** (1) The credential is a 6-digit PIN, stored as a hash. Five wrong tries lock the member out for 15 minutes. (2) The device uses the device token of O3 again, with no second pairing. (3) A tablet member takes a seat or not. This is money, so the owner decides. (4) The idle screen reads no record. (5) The audit names the device with a new `via_kind='device'`. That needs a migration, at the next free number at build time. (6) The session is a short kind of its own, and never a NextAuth cookie. (7) A route with no shell needs WS-44 (`navigation_shell.md`)

## Done when

An act with no member named writes nothing. A session ends after one act or after 60 s with no input. The audit names the member and the device. Visual review at tablet width
