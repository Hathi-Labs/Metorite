# C19

Tier: HIGH

Gate: AGENT-SAFE. After C7a

## What

**Lookup tables** (task 2350). Wave 2 (D202). A keyed object for a rate card, a price list or a material master, with an optional valid-from date. A formula (C7a) reads one row by its key

## Done when

A freight quote reads the rate of its lane and weight on the quote date. A missing key gives a clear error, never zero. R8
