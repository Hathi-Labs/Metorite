## What

**The sheet of line items** (task 2361). Wave 2 (D202). **Status: BUILT, dark 2026-11-14 (STU-D17a), behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_STUDIO_APP_FRAME`.** It is rung 4, beside AF-5. A parent object with a `lines` field (C13) gets a Lines tab on its record page, one sheet for each lines field. The model is `lib/studio/sheetModel.ts` and `lib/studio/sheetPaste.ts`. The body is `components/studio/StudioSheet.tsx`, and the cell editors that it shares with the Table are in `StudioCellEditors.tsx`. The tests are `sheetModel.test.ts`, `sheetPaste.test.ts`, `StudioSheet.test.ts`, `recordPageModel.test.ts`, `StudioRecord.test.ts` and `realDoor.test.ts`. **The save.** A cell edits into a local buffer, and no cell writes. One Save sends ONE `door.run(object:<parent>.update, {id, data: {<lines key>: [...]}})`. A new row is `{data}`, and a changed row is `{id, data}` with the changed keys only. The door saves the record and its lines in one `apply_writes` call, all or none, at most 49 lines. The app declares `object:<parent>.update` and `object:<line>.list`. No new door verb, migration, manifest key or flag exists. **The rule recipe.** "Sums to 100 %" needs no new rule kind. The parent has a roll-up (the sum of `pct` through the back-link, into `total`) and a `range` of `total` from 100 to 100. The engine refuses a breaking save with 422 `rule_refused`, and `tests/unit/test_studio_lines_r8.py` proves the refusal. A new parent with no lines saves, because `total` stays null and the range passes a null. A save that leaves lines which sum to 0 is refused. **The Lines tab.** The Related tab drops the back-link of a lines child, so the rows show once. A cell outside `INLINE_TYPES` shows read only. A computed key, the back-link and a lines field are never sent. **The refusal.** The sheet judges no rule. A refusal keeps every edit. "Line N" names the Nth line of the body that was sent, so it follows the body order and not the screen order after a sort. A rule of the parent shows as one alert over the sheet, with its place and rule prefix stripped (`parentRuleMessage`). After a save the door cache drops, and the sheet shows the `total` of the answer. Unsaved edits use `useLeaveGuard`. **The paste.** `sheetPaste.ts` reads the quoted cells, CRLF and the trailing line break of Excel and Google Sheets. It skips a header row that matches the column labels, and it maps columns from the focused column. Each cell goes through `checkValue`, and a bad cell refuses the paste as "Row 3, Pct: ...". New and changed rows together make at most 49 lines. The sheet refuses a paste over the cap before any call, and it never cuts it into chunks. A paste of more than 49 rows waits on E8. A paste of 20 rows is 20 `{data}` lines in one call. **The owner and the viewer.** Only the owner edits. A viewer (J2a) gets a read-only sheet with no Save, no paste and no new row. **Last writer wins.** A line has no version check, so a save overwrites the keys that it names. **Not built.** A delete of a line is D17c, and a grid view on a page is D17b. On a phone, DataGrid draws a blank cell of a new row with no edit button, so a new row there takes its values by paste or through its first column
