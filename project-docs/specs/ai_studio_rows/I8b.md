# I8b

Tier: HIGH

Gate: AGENT-SAFE. After I8a. A migration

## What

**Ask an admin** (task 3215). A member who may not connect presses "Ask an admin" on the line of I8a. The ask puts one Needs-you item in front of the admins of the org, and a connect clears it. A migration adds the store of an ask

## Done when

A member's ask makes one item for the admins, and a second ask makes no second item. A connect clears it. R8: an ask of org A reaches no admin of org B
