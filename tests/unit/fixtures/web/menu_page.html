Julia Pro: a large 3D printer

The Julia Pro prints large parts in one pass. It has a heated chamber, a hardened steel nozzle and a bed of 400 by 400 millimetres. The machine runs for many hours with no operator at its side.

Materials

The printer reads PLA, PETG, ABS and nylon with carbon fibre. A dry box keeps each spool at a low humidity, so the parts stay strong and clean.

  • Bed: 400 x 400 mm
  • Chamber: 70 degrees Celsius

Each machine ships with a calibration report and two years of support from the engineers who built it.
