## 1 · Named speakers
Name Deepgram's anonymous `S1`/`S2`/`S3` once → back-fills transcript, notes, action owners, follow-up email. `101_note_taker_speaker_names.sql`; `PUT /meetings/{id}/speakers`; resolved at display + prompt time; raw labels never rewritten (re-transcription stays idempotent).

## 2 · Summary-first workspace
Cramped two-column grid → **tabbed workspace**: meta strip (date · duration · speakers · language · model) over **Summary** (notes hero + scratch) · **Transcript** · **Actions** (count) · **Ask**. Ask citations jump to the transcript across tabs.

## 3 · Live transcription
Captions during recording via Deepgram's streaming WebSocket — **browser-direct** (gateway stays out of the audio path, D7). `POST /stt/live-token` mints a ~60 s scoped key; `lib/live.ts` + `recorder.ts` stream 16 kHz PCM; graceful fallback to the authoritative batch re-pass. ⚠️ The socket path needs a real Deepgram key + mic to confirm at runtime (built to fail safe).

## 4 · Meeting templates
The notes template is now user-pickable, so a meeting is cut through the right lens. `templates.py` 2 → **6** (added **1:1, customer/sales call, interview, retrospective**). A **template dropdown** in the Summary tab PATCHes `template_key` and regenerates.
