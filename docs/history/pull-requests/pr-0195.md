## Description

## What & why

Following up on the "working hours vs 24h" feedback: instead of two views behind a toggle, the calendar now works the way Google Calendar does — **one grid that always renders the full 24-hour day**. You can view and schedule at any hour without switching modes.

Working hours stop being a wall and become a *soft zone*:
- the hours **outside** your working window read dimmer (a subtle shade), so the day still has a natural shape;
- the working window stays the sensible **default** that the AI planner, auto-place, and roll-over fill;
- **direct manipulation works everywhere** — tap an empty slot, drag a block, or resize it into the early morning or late night whenever you want.

On open (and when you switch to day/week) the grid **auto-scrolls to ~1h before now**, so you land where the action is instead of at midnight.

## Changes

- **`CalendarView.tsx`** — remove the `fullDay` / `toggleFullDay` state and the `24h` toggle button; pass `TimeGrid` a fixed `0–24` extent plus the working window as `workStart` / `workEnd`; auto-scroll to now on mount / mode switch; route schedule / roll-over / plan defaults through the working window.
- **`calendar/TimeGrid.tsx`** — accept `workStart` / `workEnd`; render a subtle non-working-hours shade above and below the working window.
- **`lib/focusPrefs.ts`** — drop the now-unused `fullDayGrid` preference.
