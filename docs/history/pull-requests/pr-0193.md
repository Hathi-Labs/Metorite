## Description

Entrepreneurs work whenever they want, not just 7am–10pm. The grid and planner were boxed into the configured working-hours window; this adds a **24h** toggle so you can plan across the full day.

## What it does
- A **`24h` button** in the calendar header expands the day/week grid to the full **0–24** range. In this mode you can view, **drag, tap-schedule, resize, and first-free-slot at any hour** — 2am deep-work session, 11pm calls, whatever fits your life.
- The **AI planner spans the whole day** in 24h mode too: capacity still caps *how much* it books, energy windows + your planning philosophy still bias *where*, and free time starts from **now** — so "Plan my day" at 8pm fills the evening instead of refusing.
- **Off = the usual working-hours window** (default). The preference persists.
- The grid **auto-scrolls to the current hour** when you enable 24h, so you don't land at midnight.

## Implementation
- `fullDayGrid` persisted in `focusPrefs`, resolved in the SSR-safe effect.
- Effective `dayStart`/`dayEnd` become `0`/`24` when on — and since the grid, manual scheduling, rollover, and the plan panel all already read `dayStart`/`dayEnd`, the whole surface picks it up with no other wiring.
