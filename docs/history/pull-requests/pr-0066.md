## Description

P1 from the context/output-budgeting review. Follows #65 (independent files — no conflict).

## Problem

The model-aware assembler (`assemble_run_context`) is correct — but several executor paths **bypassed it** with hardcoded caps that have no relationship to the context window:

| path | cap | share of a 128k window |
|---|---|---|
| `_build_event_message` | last 16 msgs × 600 chars | **~1.9%** |
| Copilot new-session inject | last 30 × 600 | ~3.5% |
| Copilot stale-retry inject | last 30 × 800 | ~4.7% |

So a large-context model was fed **a few KB of history regardless of holding 128k–1M tokens**.

Two aggravating details:
- The **600-char slice also hit the immediately-preceding turn** — a user who pasted a document, diff, or error log had it cut to 600 chars on the very next message.
- The two Copilot ones run on **every session rebuild** (e.g. after a gateway restart), so they decide how much a resumed chat remembers. The new-session block's own comment claimed a *"larger budget"* because diffs/file contents are *"meaningless when cut to a few sentences"* — while cutting them to 600 chars.

## Fix

All three now share `_render_history_block`, budgeting from `context_window_for(model)`:
- oldest turns drop first (mirrors the assembler's whole-turn eviction),
- a single oversized turn is trimmed rather than allowed to eat the budget,
- fails open to a floor when the window can't be resolved (never raises).

**Measured:**

| tier | window | old budget | new budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| tier-powerful | 131,072 tok | 9,600 chars (~2,400 tok) | **262,144 chars (~65,536 tok)** — 27× |
| tier-fast | 1,000,000 tok | 9,600 chars | **2,000,000 chars** |

A 60-turn thread now keeps **60/60 turns** where the old cap kept 16 shredded ones.

Also **drops the `is_byok` gate** on `_compose_maf_run_input`: a non-BYOK native-MAF agent's *streaming* turn fell back to the flat string prompt while its own *batch* turn got the token-budgeted assembly — the same agent remembered a different amount of the conversation depending on whether it streamed.

## Deliberately not changed
The Tier-2 batch fallback's `[-20:]` is a **count-only** cap (no char truncation) in a rarely-hit path with a documented provider-specific rationale — left as-is to keep the blast radius tight.
