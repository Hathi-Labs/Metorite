## Description

Audio used to wait for a button press *after* the call connected. That left the caller in silence through the part of a call where feedback matters most — and silence was indistinguishable from the audio being broken, which is exactly the ambiguity that made the last two bugs take three rounds to find.

## Change

- **Mic opens first**, inside the click that places the call. `getUserMedia` needs user activation, and spending that activation on a network round-trip loses it — so `prepare()` (mic + audio graph) and `attach()` (socket) are now separate phases.
- **Local ringback starts immediately** and stops the instant real audio arrives from the far end. Pressing Call is audible straight away.
- **The socket joins as soon as the call has an id**, while it's still ringing — no waiting on a phase transition, which is what previously left the caller with no way in at all.

## Failure modes handled

- **Mic refused** → the call still places; that leg is simply listen-only. Denial doesn't block calling.
- **Call fails to place** → audio tears down, rather than leaving a tone playing into a failure with the microphone still open.
- The button remains for joining a call this page didn't place (e.g. an inbound one you answered), relabelled **Join audio**.

## Note on the ringback

It's generated locally, not WhatsApp's. Whether the far end's real ringback arrives as early media is up to WhatsApp; if it does, it replaces the local tone immediately. The tone exists so "still ringing" and "audio is broken" stop sounding the same.
