## Description

Refines the prioritization matrix per feedback: labels describe the priority **character** (not the action), and the Priority + Engage views group by those labels.

## Labels lose the action-words → 7 levels
"Delegate / Schedule / Eliminate" now live only in the competing card badge, never in a level name. Stripping them collapses the two "not important to you" cases (urgent-only + neither) into one **Low Priority** level — 7 levels, not 8:

| # | Inputs | Label | Badge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lev+Imp+Urg | 🔥 Critical | — |
| 2 | Imp+Urg | 🚨 Urgent | Delegate? |
| 3 | Lev+Imp | 📈 High-Leverage | — |
| 4 | Imp | ❗ Important | Schedule? |
| 5 | Lev+Urg | 📤 Quick Leverage Win | — |
| 6 | Lev | 🧪 Speculative Bet | — |
| 7 | Urg-only **or** neither | 🗑 Low Priority | Eliminate? |

`cell_for_inputs` maps both urgent-only and neither → `low-priority`. The Low Priority badge reads "Eliminate?" → *eliminate it, or delegate it if it must happen*. `priority.ts` ⟷ `priority.py` kept byte-parity.

## Views
- **Priority view** is grouped by level again (ranked sections) — reverting the flat list from the previous revision.
- **Engage view** is now grouped by level too (was a flat ranked list).
- The **suggestion badge** now shows in the open task (ItemDetail Priority section / TaskFocusModal), not just on cards.
