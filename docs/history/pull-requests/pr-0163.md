## Description

## Why

User audit request: "Is my digest working? It doesn''t provide correct information about due commitments, pending replies, and the actions I should take." The audit found the action half broken, and this PR builds the requested **Dashboard** on top of the fixed data.

## Truth fixes (verified against the live DB)

- **Replying can no longer leave you "owing a reply"** — `recompute_thread_status` clamps a REPLY verdict to AWAITING when the *real* last message is our side (fact over judgment; reason discloses the coercion). 10 of 29 live NEEDS_REPLY rows were threads the user had already answered.
- **Trashed/junked threads leave the ledgers** — a trashed thread sat in "awaiting your reply" for weeks.
- **Undated commitments become visible** — 3 of the user''s 4 open promises had no due date and were filtered out.

## Dashboard (one computation, two projections)

- `_generate_digest(full=True)` = the in-app projection: full lists (cap 50) with `thread_id`/`message_id`/`who`, the **Waiting on them** (AWAITING) ledger, undated commitments. The scheduled email keeps its small-cap brief (+ a waiting-on-them section).
- `DashboardView` (feature key stays `digest`): **Needs your reply** and **Waiting on them** ledgers — every row opens its conversation via `openEmailById`, with row actions **Mark done** (`/reply-zero/resolve`, closes captured tasks too) and **Snooze 1d**; commitments; category + noisy-sender analytics; 5-stat header (adds Waiting-on-them).
- Sidebar/header: **Digest → Dashboard**.
