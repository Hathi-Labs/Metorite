## Description

Per the preferred UX: instead of auto-cramming overdue-incomplete tasks onto today, **roll-over now releases them back to your unscheduled list** so you re-plan them deliberately (drag, or Rebuild my day). Applied everywhere roll-over happens:

- **Nightly auto job** (`_rollover_one_user`): clears the schedule of overdue *flexible* incomplete blocks once per local day (logged as released), instead of packing them onto today.
- **Browser endpoint** `/calendar/rollover`: returns the overdue tasks as `evicted` (no blocks) — the client apply clears their times and they land in the unscheduled rail. Removed the now-dead `_pack_rollover` + `_TODAY_BUSY_WHERE`.
- **Manual overdue banner + startup-ritual carry-forward**: now an instant, undoable client-side release — **"Move to my list"**, no server round-trip.
- **Agent** `gtd_rollover` + `_resolve_agent_plan`: propose/apply works for an evict-only plan (`has_changes = blocks OR evicted`); docstring + persona reframed to "returns overdue to the list."
- **Settings** toggle relabelled **"Auto-return unfinished tasks to my list daily"** with a one-line explanation.

Fixed (🔒) meetings are still never touched. Rebuild my day still folds these back into a plan on demand (from the list, or via the prior-day carry-forward sweep) — so nothing is lost, and the day only fills when you ask it to.

## Why
Auto-placing overdue work onto the next day silently re-crams the calendar and takes control away. Returning it to the list keeps you in charge of when things get scheduled — and pairs cleanly with Rebuild (which pulls from the list).
